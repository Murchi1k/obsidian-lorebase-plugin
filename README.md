## ✨ What is Lorebase?

**Lorebase** turns your [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) vault into a media library with cover cards, metadata, personal ratings, progress, filters and statistics. Each item is a Markdown note with YAML properties, so you can also read and edit your collection outside the plugin.

**🆕 Version 4.0.0** brings music (beta), AniList, Trakt and PlayStation sync, a release calendar, custom groups, flexible ratings, a bookshelf view and redesigned statistics.

> **💾 Your collection lives in your vault.** Add and edit notes manually, or connect online services for metadata and synchronization. Use **Additional online features** in General settings to choose whether to show galleries, actors, recommendations and music discovery.

## 🚀 Features

### Seven libraries

Each library has its own folder and note template. Appearance and available fields depend on the media type.

| Library | Metadata and tracking |
|---|---|
| **Games** | Game/visual novel type, developers, publishers, series, DLC, platforms, replay counts, completion percentage, time, achievements, game modes and HowLongToBeat estimates. |
| **Anime** | Country of origin, adult flag, TV/movie/OVA/ONA/special format, studios, alternative titles, parts, seasons, episodes and rewatches. |
| **Movies** | Movie/animation/documentary type, runtime, directors, cast, optional parts, viewing dates and rewatches. |
| **TV shows** | Series/animated series/documentary series/TV show type, seasons and parts, episode progress, cast and rewatches. |
| **Books** | Authors, publisher, pages and chapters, reading dates, reading cycles, cover effect and bookshelf view. |
| **Manga** | Manga, manhwa, manhua, comics and custom types, authors/artists, alternative titles, chapters, volumes, reading cycles and rereads. |
| **Music (beta)** | Albums and individual tracks, artists, disc-grouped tracklists, listened marks, track ratings/favorites, duration and square/vinyl/CD covers. |

### 🎨 Make statuses your own

- 🏷️ **Rename statuses** — use the labels that fit your collection.
- 🎨 **Choose colors and Lucide icons** — recognize each status at a glance.
- ➕ **Add custom statuses** — create your own stages for playing, watching, reading or listening.
- 🗂️ **Customize each library separately** — game statuses can look different from anime, books or music.

### 🏷️ Types and countries

- **Games, movies, series and manga:** add, rename, reorder or remove work types, with reassignment for existing entries.
- **Anime:** country controls and a separate TV/movie/OVA/ONA/special format field.
- **Card badges and filters:** use your types and countries to browse the collection. Books and music use their own media fields.

### Adding, importing and editing

- **Unified add window** — Search enabled providers, preview selected results and see which titles are already in your library.
- **Manual creation:** enter metadata and personal details, upload a cover or use a URL. Tab changes retain your input.
- **Existing-note import** — Map or exclude fields, preview changes, then copy or update and move selected notes. Import supports all seven libraries, including albums and tracks.
- **Inbox from tagged lists** — Tag a list note with `#lorebase` or `#LB`, then open **Add → Inbox**. Review the titles, choose a catalog match or prepare an entry manually, and add the selected titles to your library.
- **Editors:** manage metadata, personal notes, dates, status, rating, progress and related local media. **Updated editor** in General settings switches between the new compact layout and the previous layout.
- **Personal notes** — Use **How I found it** to record who recommended a title and why it caught your interest. Turn on its card badge to see which titles have a discovery note. Use **Why I stopped** or **Why I paused** to record the reason when you drop a title or put it on hold.
- **Mobile layout:** General, Personal and More tabs with a bottom save bar. Mobile UI remains under active development; some services have desktop requirements listed below.
- **Metadata sources:** link an existing card, change its provider and refresh metadata. Personal tracking and manually edited fields are preserved by the refresh flow.
- **Source galleries:** view available posters, backdrops, images and trailers from the editor cover. Availability and playback depend on the provider.
- **Related media:** link local games, anime, movies, series, books and manga. Books and manga can also form ordered reading cycles.

### Progress and ratings

Anime parts and TV seasons support counters or individually checked episodes in grid/list views, including out-of-order viewing and provider episode names when available. Parts keep their own titles, formats, years, covers, ratings, statuses and progress. The poster can follow the active part or stay pinned to the title cover. Viewing duration estimates use episode counts and runtime; they are estimates, not measured viewing time.

If you already watched a title, choose **Completed** in its card menu. When prompted for the completion date, enter the year or the year and month if that is all you remember. If you cannot remember when, choose **Date unknown**.

Games support manual completion percentage, hours/minutes, achievement totals, solo/co-op/multiplayer modes and multiple played-on platforms with replay counts. Steam can refresh supported time/achievement data; PlayStation uses its own playtime and trophy data.

Personal ratings offer:

- **Classic:** 1–5 scores, star/emoji presentation and standard, themed, seasonal or holiday presets.
- **Custom:** maximum from 2 to 10, steps of 1, 0.5 or 0.1, and number/star/custom-icon presentation.
- **Detailed:** separate scores for your own criteria.
- **Community:** provider score and vote count, separate from your personal rating. An optional card badge uses a community, star or tomato icon.

### Search, filters and collections

Search checks title and part names and their alternative titles. It tolerates word order, case and punctuation; Russian ё/е are treated alike. Exact matches rank before prefixes and other matches. A matching part opens through its Part link without duplicating the title card.

Studio suggestions for anime, author suggestions for books/manga and developer suggestions for games can become filters. Advanced filters cover supported system and custom YAML fields, lists, numbers and dates, including renamed template properties. Sort, group and save views per library; use custom groups for selected collections.

Set a title's **Priority** in the editor or card menu. Choose **Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Highest**, then sort, group or filter by priority in the library.

The **release calendar** shows known dates from existing game, anime, movie, series, book and manga notes, with calendar/agenda modes and media filters. It is not a feed of every upcoming release; music is not included. Awaiting-release statuses and countdowns use the dates already stored in your notes.

### Cards and appearance

- Vertical and horizontal layouts, size presets and custom dimensions.
- Independent desktop/phone grid sizing by card width or column count, with spacing controls and a grid preview.
- Interactive previews using library entries or sample cards: drag text and badges, toggle elements and adjust description lines.
- Configurable status, personal/community rating, favorite and type/country badges, where supported.
- Hover overlays or progress-footer styles where supported; optional scrolling for long descriptions.
- Cover effects for books/manga, a book-only bookshelf view, and square/vinyl/CD music covers.
- Completion-date formatting for supported cards; the completion-date badge option does not apply to music.
- Accent presets/custom color and Sakura, Snow, Leaves, Fireflies or Rain effects. Intensity ranges from **5 to 80**, with a separate option for animation when reduced motion is requested.

### Music discovery and previews

Search **MusicBrainz**, **Last.fm** or **Deezer**, or create albums/tracks manually. MusicBrainz artwork uses Cover Art Archive. Album tracks can have listened marks, ratings, favorites and performer credits, and can be added as separate linked track notes. Album notes include a rendered tracklist.

Open artist discographies and available albums, EPs, singles or credited releases. Last.fm listener/scrobble counts are source statistics, not a record of your own listening. These views require internet and use temporary caches; browsing alone does not create notes.

The music editor supports **up to 30-second Deezer previews** when a matching clip is available, with play/pause, seek and volume controls. This is not full-track streaming or a local audio player.

### People and recommendations

Movie/series cast cards open TMDB person profiles and filmographies, with explicit actions to add titles. Similar-title recommendations are available for anime, movies and series from supported sources. Existing titles open their notes; new notes are created only when you choose to add them. These features need internet, and results depend on the selected source.

### Statistics

A shared dashboard covers all seven libraries with an overview, statuses, personal ratings, genres, a country map, completion activity and media-specific time/progress. Select supported chart data to inspect matching titles. Move and resize blocks, toggle visibility, and change density, cover size and activity period.

Statistics use your local notes and tracking fields: estimated viewing time, reported game time, music durations and reading progress in pages/chapters. These values have different sources and should not be interpreted as one measured usage timer.

1. Download **main.js**, **manifest.json** and **styles.css** from the **same version** on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Murchi1k/obsidian-lorebase-plugin/releases). For 4.0.0, choose its release once published, or build this checkout.

   ~~~text
   <Vault>/.obsidian/plugins/lorebase/
   ├── main.js
   ├── manifest.json
   └── styles.css
   ~~~

~~~

The build produces **main.js** and **styles.css** in the repository root. Install them together with this checkout's **manifest.json** using the manual instructions. `npm run dev` watches TypeScript and CSS during development.

Templates offer a searchable field table and simple/advanced modes. Renaming a YAML property keeps its value mapping: for example, `name: "{{VALUE:name}}"` can become `title: "{{VALUE:name}}"`. Keep the value marker when renaming an advanced-template field.

Supported fields can be written as Obsidian wikilinks using the link control or a marker such as `{{VALUE:authors|wikilink}}`. Support is field-specific.

**Compact mode** omits empty managed fields while preserving zero and false. Filling a field through the editor inserts it in template order; clearing it removes the empty field. Custom properties and the note body are preserved.

## 📝 Data format

These are **illustrative partial notes**, not complete default templates or live provider records. Names, dates, totals and local image paths are sample values. Select the properties you need in Templates; imported/provider notes may contain additional metadata and sync fields.

Keep notes in the corresponding configured library folder. Default properties vary by media: games use `name` and `userRating`; other libraries use `title` and `rating`. Anime uses `image`/`image_b`, while other cover templates use `poster`/`poster_b`. Default video templates use `directors`. Progress commonly uses snake_case, but fields such as `userRating`, `gameSeries` and `communityRating` retain their documented spelling.

Personal and community ratings are separate: `communityRating` stores a provider score normalized to **0–100**, with `communityVotes` and `communityRatingProvider` where available. Books/manga display Reading in the UI but store `status: watching`. Music stores `listening` instead.

Cards use reflow animations when motion is enabled. The system reduced-motion preference disables these transitions.
